## Chapter 5: Domain 3: Prompt Engineering Techniques & Inference Parameters — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Temperature value for identical outputs every time (deterministic)? | Temperature: 0 Same input always produces same output. Exam tip: Use for customer service, policy Q&A, legal documents, or any scenario requiring consistency/reproducibility. |
| 2 | Temperature value for maximum creativity and variation? | Temperature: 1.0 Maximum randomness and diverse outputs. Exam tip: Use for creative writing, brainstorming, generating unique content (e.g., 50 different product descriptions). |
| 3 | What is Top-p (nucleus sampling) and typical value? | Top-p: 0.9 = consider tokens in top 90% probability mass Higher Top-p = more diversity. Exam tip: Use with high temperature for creative tasks. Lower Top-p (0.1-0.3) with Temperature 0 for consistency. |
| 4 | What is Top-k and when does it restrict vocabulary? | Top-k: N = consider only top N tokens at each step Lower values (10-20) restrict vocabulary to most common/formal terms. Higher values (50+) allow diverse vocabulary. Exam tip: Cannot use Top-p and Top-k simultaneously. |
| 5 | Zero-shot prompting: definition and example? | No examples provided, just instruction Example: 'Translate to French: Hello' Exam tip: Works for common tasks the model already understands. If accuracy is low, switch to few-shot. |
| 6 | Few-shot prompting: definition and optimal number of examples? | Provide 2-5 examples before the actual task Example: 'English: cat, French: chat. English: dog, French: chien. English: bird, French: ?' Exam tip: 2-5 examples is optimal. 10+ examples wastes tokens and doesn't improve accuracy much. |
| 7 | When to use chain-of-thought prompting? | Multi-step reasoning tasks where you need to see the logic Instruction: 'Let's think step by step' or 'Show your work' Exam tip: Use for math problems, medical diagnosis, complex analysis. NOT for creative writing or simple classification. |
| 8 | Max tokens: 512 equals approximately how many words? | ~400 words (tokens ≈ 0.75 × words) Exam tip: Max tokens limits OUTPUT length, not input. Common values: 256 (short), 512 (medium), 1024 (long), 2048 (very long). |
| 9 | Customer service chatbot needs consistent answers. Temperature and Top-p values? | Temperature: 0, Top-p: 0.1 Exam tip: Consistency scenarios always use Temperature 0. Low Top-p (0.1-0.3) further restricts variation. Also applies to: policy docs, legal text, reproducible outputs. |
| 10 | Creative marketing content needs variety. Temperature and Top-p values? | Temperature: 0.8-1.0, Top-p: 0.9 Exam tip: Creative scenarios need high Temperature (0.8+) for variation. High Top-p (0.9) enables diverse word choices. Also applies to: brainstorming, unique descriptions, creative writing. |
| 11 | Model can do calculations but makes logical errors. Which prompting technique fixes this? | Chain-of-thought prompting Add: 'Let's solve this step by step: 1) Identify knowns, 2) Identify unknowns, 3) Choose operation, 4) Calculate' Exam tip: Chain-of-thought fixes REASONING errors, not calculation errors. |
| 12 | Translate technical docs with specialized terms. Zero-shot vs few-shot? | Few-shot with 2-3 examples showing technical term translations Exam tip: Zero-shot works for general translation. Few-shot needed when: specialized vocabulary, specific style/format, or domain-specific accuracy required. |
